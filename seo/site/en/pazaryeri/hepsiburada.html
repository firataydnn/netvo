FAQ

How much is Hepsiburada's commission?

Hepsiburada commission ranges from %6–19.49 by category. VAT is added on top of the rate.

How much is really deducted per sale on Hepsiburada?

On the advertised 15.4% commission, adding VAT and fixed fees brings the total marketplace deduction to about 19%.

What is the VAT rate for Hepsiburada?

Türkiye standard VAT is 20%. Commission base: price incl. VAT.
