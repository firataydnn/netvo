New ranking tracks which retailers AI is helping the most

20 Ağu 2026

The AI Commerce Rankings measure online stores' visibility across AI channels.

The AI Commerce Rankings, launched in July by Digital Commerce 360 and ReFiBuy, represent a systematic effort to measure how effectively artificial intelligence is reshaping the e-commerce landscape. This ranking framework tracks online retailers' visibility and performance across AI-powered channels such as ChatGPT, Google Shopping, Amazon's AI features, and similar platforms where consumer discovery increasingly originates. Unlike traditional search engines, these emerging channels fundamentally alter how products are discovered and recommended, making visibility in these spaces critical for sales performance. The measurement extends beyond simple visibility metrics to include conversion rates, click-through performance, and the frequency with which products appear in AI-generated recommendations.

For e-commerce businesses, these rankings carry substantial strategic implications. Retailers can now understand precisely where their products appear in AI search results and how frequently they convert compared to competitors. This data enables smaller and mid-sized merchants to compete more effectively against larger brands by optimizing their product information, descriptions, and availability specifically for AI algorithms. As artificial intelligence continues to expand its role in online shopping—from search suggestions to personalized purchasing recommendations—companies that understand and adapt to these new visibility dynamics will gain competitive advantage. Monitoring performance in AI channels is rapidly becoming as essential as traditional search engine optimization.
