Thailand: Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada to cut seller fees by up to 43%

October 30, 2026

Under a deal with the Commerce Ministry, fees fall from 1 December 2026 to 31 May 2027, covering about 1.2 million traders.

Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada have agreed to a Thai Commerce Ministry scheme to cut mandatory sales fees to the levels in force on 1 January, Nation Thailand reports. The cuts run from 1 December 2026 to 31 May 2027. Current maximum rates are 19.26% at Shopee, 21.94% at TikTok Shop and 17.50% at Lazada; the new ranges are 8.03–13.91%, 11.24–17.66% and 7.50–11.60% respectively. The biggest cuts are 42.9% at Lazada, 35% at TikTok Shop and 33.3% at Shopee. About 1.02 million individual operators with annual revenue up to THB1.8 million and roughly 180,000 legal entities up to THB3.5 million can benefit, provided they have completed e-commerce registration. Combined savings are estimated at about THB1.93 billion.

Background: the platforms raised fees three times earlier in 2026, drawing many complaints, and the ministry calls the cut a short-term measure. Lesson for Southeast Asia sellers: commission rates can become a regulatory issue, and temporary cuts should not be baked into margin plans.
