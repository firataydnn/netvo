Amazon expands shopping features across Prime Video

10 Eyl 2026

Amazon is rolling out new tools that let Prime Video viewers discover and purchase products featured in thousands of shows, movies, and live sports events through X-Ray, its shopping app, and an AI-powered 'Shop the Scene' feature.

Amazon is deepening shopping integration within its Prime Video platform. The company has introduced new capabilities that enable viewers to purchase products they see in films, series, and live sports broadcasts directly from their mobile devices. The integration includes X-Ray functionality, Amazon's shopping application, and a new artificial intelligence-powered feature called 'Shop the Scene' that leverages Lens technology.

This move reflects a broader trend in which content and retail are converging at an accelerating pace. Shopping integration within social media platforms and streaming applications has emerged as one of the fastest-growing commercial models in recent years. Services like TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube have already invested heavily in this direction, and Amazon is now seeking to convert its own content ecosystem—Prime Video—into a sales funnel.

Amazon's strategy amounts to transforming the visual elements of thousands of productions into commercial opportunities. Clothing worn by an actor in a series, furniture visible in the background, or equipment used by an athlete in a live sports broadcast can instantly become a purchasable link. The Lens technology automates this process by identifying objects in moving images and recommending corresponding products.

For sellers and brands, this development presents a mixed picture. On one hand, having a product appear naturally in a show, film, or sports broadcast can be a more credible and effective sales trigger than traditional advertising. On the other hand, such integrations establish major platform operators as all-encompassing ecosystems—not just the products you buy, but everything you watch potentially becomes a sales channel. Participating sellers may gain greater visibility, but competition on the platform also intensifies. In the long term, features like these could become a primary driver of Amazon's sales growth and help the company differentiate itself from competitors.
