Michaels launches a 3D, augmented-reality frame builder

20 Ağu 2026

The retailer uses 3D/AR in shopping to let customers preview a product before buying.

Michaels, a leading retailer in the hobby and arts-and-crafts category, is investing in 3D and augmented-reality technology to transform the digital shopping experience. The new frame builder tool allows customers to create their own customized frames in a virtual environment and see different materials, colors, and styling options in real time. Such interactive tools can reduce pre-purchase uncertainty, potentially boosting conversion rates and lowering return rates by helping shoppers make more confident decisions before committing to a purchase.

Augmented reality is becoming increasingly common in retail, particularly in categories like home décor, furniture, and customizable products where visual decision-making is critical. Michaels' move aims to transport the experiential shopping expectations of physical stores into the online channel. Customers can evaluate how a product will look in their homes or a specific context before buying, resulting in more informed purchasing decisions and higher customer satisfaction. This approach also creates an opportunity to reduce buyer's remorse, a significant pain point in e-commerce.

For e-commerce merchants, these trends signal that technology investment is now a core component of competitive differentiation. Especially in creative categories like DIY and crafts, AR and 3D previews not only boost customer confidence but also reinforce a brand's innovative positioning. Retailers that embrace such tools early may find themselves with a meaningful advantage as consumer expectations around digital shopping experiences continue to evolve.
