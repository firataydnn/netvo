Glass brings AI features to its government procurement marketplace

19 Ağu 2026

The company is relaunching its G-Commerce marketplace for government agencies with AI upgrades.

Glass, a government-procurement technology company, has publicly released multiple AI features for G-Commerce, its marketplace. The company frames the move as a reintroduction of G-Commerce for the government agencies that use it. The development points to AI's growing role in business-to-government (B2G) e-commerce.
