Shopify highlights AI-powered search driving 80% conversion uplift for merchants

14 Eyl 2026

Shopify announced that agentic AI technology integrated into its search and product discovery capabilities has delivered an 80% conversion boost, while lowering barriers for small and medium-sized businesses and strengthening payment infras

Shopify disclosed that its AI-powered search functionality is driving an 80% increase in conversion rates, according to executives speaking at a Goldman Sachs technology conference in September. The company's CFO and VP of Product highlighted how agentic artificial intelligence is reshaping digital commerce discovery while reducing startup friction for SMB sellers and reinforcing the platform's payment and checkout systems.

The platform has redesigned its core search experience by leveraging large language models to make product discovery faster and more intuitive for shoppers. The technology analyzes buying behavior and delivers personalized product recommendations, aiming to enhance customer satisfaction and accelerate the sales cycle.

This move aligns with a broader industry trend toward optimizing search and recommendation engines through artificial intelligence. Major platforms including Amazon and Alibaba are similarly investing in AI-driven product discovery systems. Shopify's initiative positions the platform competitively by giving its merchants access to this technology without requiring them to build proprietary systems.

For sellers, improved search functionality can boost sales potential by enhancing customer experience and reducing cart abandonment. Small and medium-sized businesses especially benefit from adopting Shopify's ready-made AI search solution rather than developing and maintaining custom discovery systems in-house, reducing operational costs and complexity.

Through AI capabilities and payment infrastructure enhancements, Shopify aims to strengthen merchant loyalty and increase transaction volume across its ecosystem. The move underscores the platform's strategy to empower smaller retailers to compete more effectively with larger e-commerce incumbents.
