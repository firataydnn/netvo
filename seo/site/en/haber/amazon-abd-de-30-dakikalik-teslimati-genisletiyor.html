Amazon expands 30-minute delivery across the US

12 May 2026

Amazon is rolling out its ultra-fast delivery service nationwide, covering thousands of items including fresh groceries and household essentials.

Amazon's nationwide rollout of 30-minute delivery marks a significant escalation in the race for speed that now defines modern e-commerce competition. The service encompasses thousands of items spanning fresh groceries, household products, express parcels, and locally sourced goods—demonstrating the scale of logistics infrastructure required to fulfill such promises. This expansion is underpinned by Amazon's substantial capital investments in warehouse networks, fleet operations, and last-mile delivery capabilities built over several years.

The shift reflects a fundamental change in consumer expectations. What was once considered expedited two-day shipping now feels sluggish to many customers, creating competitive pressure that forces platforms to innovate aggressively in logistics technology and operational efficiency. Speed has become a primary differentiator alongside price and selection, pushing companies to reimagine their supply chains entirely. This intensifies the broader industry trend where delivery velocity directly influences market share and customer retention.

For third-party sellers and e-commerce businesses, the implications are multifaceted. Merchants capable of maintaining sufficient inventory and partnering effectively with Amazon's logistics network stand to gain increased visibility within the platform's fastest-growing service tier. Conversely, smaller sellers unable to support such rapid fulfillment timelines face mounting pressure to either invest heavily in logistics infrastructure or risk diminishing competitiveness as customer preferences increasingly favor ultra-fast options.
