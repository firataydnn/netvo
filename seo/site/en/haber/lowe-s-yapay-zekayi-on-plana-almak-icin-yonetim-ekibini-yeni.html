Lowe's restructures management to prioritize AI across retail operations

10 Eyl 2026

Major US home improvement retailer Lowe's elevated artificial intelligence in its corporate strategy through September executive appointments, adding AI responsibilities to its Chief Information Officer's role.

Lowe's Companies elevated artificial intelligence within its organizational structure through a series of senior management appointments announced in September. Among these changes, the company expanded the role of Chief Information Officer Seemantini Godbole to include explicit AI oversight. The move underscores how a leading home improvement retailer is positioning AI as central to its competitive strategy.

The retailer has extended the application of AI tools across both consumer-facing operations and its professional customer segment (Pro). This dual-channel approach reflects a broader industry shift in which retailers deploy machine learning and AI to enhance customer experience, optimize inventory management, and streamline operational processes. Lowe's decision to formalize AI governance at the C-suite level signals the strategic importance it now assigns to these technologies.

AI integration in retail—spanning recommendation engines, demand forecasting, warehouse automation, and dynamic pricing—has become a standard competitive lever. Lowe's structural reorganization aligns with moves by peers such as Amazon and Walmart, each investing heavily in AI infrastructure. For e-commerce operators and omnichannel retailers, the trend underscores the expectation that AI competency is increasingly essential to margin defense and customer retention in a crowded market.
