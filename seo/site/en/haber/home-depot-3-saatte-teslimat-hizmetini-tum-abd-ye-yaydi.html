Home Depot expands 3-hour express delivery nationwide across US

21 Ağu 2026

Home Depot now offers 3-hour or faster delivery for thousands of products across the entire United States, leveraging over 2,000 stores as fulfillment hubs.

Home Depot has rolled out its 3-hour express delivery service across the entire United States by transforming more than 2,000 retail locations into fulfillment hubs. The service spans thousands of product SKUs across multiple categories, leveraging the company's extensive geographic footprint to accelerate last-mile logistics. This represents a tangible example of how brick-and-mortar retailers can repurpose their physical infrastructure as a competitive advantage in the e-commerce arena.

The expansion reflects a broader shift in consumer expectations around delivery speed. Major e-commerce platforms have set new baseline standards for rapid fulfillment, forcing traditional retailers like Home Depot to innovate with their existing store networks rather than build costly new distribution centers. By converting retail locations into order fulfillment points, the company reduces delivery times while optimizing underutilized store space during off-peak hours.

For Home Depot merchants and suppliers, this development carries both opportunity and operational implications. Faster delivery options can drive incremental sales and customer retention, though they also introduce complexity around inventory allocation, staffing, and real-time order management across distributed fulfillment nodes. As this model matures, the competitive pressure on traditional retail networks to offer comparable speed will likely intensify across the sector.
