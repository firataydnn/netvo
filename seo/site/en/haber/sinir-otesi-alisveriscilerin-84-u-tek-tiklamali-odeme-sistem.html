84% of Cross-Border Shoppers Cite Fast Checkout as Decisive Factor in Merchant Selection

7 Tem 2026

New PYMNTS Intelligence and Worldpay research reveals that streamlined payment experience directly influences customer choice and transaction volume in international e-commerce.

For merchants targeting overseas markets, the checkout page has become a decisive competitive battleground. According to a new report from PYMNTS Intelligence conducted in partnership with Worldpay, titled "Payments Optimization: Powering Global eCommerce Growth," cross-border demand is substantial, yet many sellers continue to complicate the purchasing process unnecessarily. The research emphasizes that one-click checkout capability significantly sways customer merchant selection decisions.

Cross-border e-commerce represents one of the fastest-growing segments in global digital commerce, yet payment frictions remain a persistent barrier to conversion. International transactions frequently suffer from multiple verification layers, limited payment method options, and lengthy form completion processes that drive customer abandonment. The report highlights that while one-click checkout has become standard expectation in mature markets (North America, Western Europe), it continues to function as a competitive differentiator in emerging markets where adoption remains uneven.

For merchants, these findings underscore that investing in streamlined payment infrastructure for cross-border transactions is no longer optional—it is strategic necessity. One-click systems that incorporate stored payment credentials, geolocation verification, and integrated local payment rails can directly boost conversion metrics. Particularly for direct-to-consumer brands and mid-market platforms entering new geographies, payment experience optimization translates immediately to revenue gain. Looking ahead, the balance between AI-powered fraud prevention and speed will define competitiveness in cross-border commerce.
