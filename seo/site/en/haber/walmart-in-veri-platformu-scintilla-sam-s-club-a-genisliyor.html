Walmart extends its Scintilla data platform to Sam's Club

20 Ağu 2026

Merchants and brands will be able to use it to understand and predict member behavior.

Walmart is extending its Scintilla data-analytics platform to Sam's Club. Sam's Club merchants and brands will be able to use it to better understand and predict member behavior. The move underscores the growing importance of retail media and first-party-data tools for sellers.
