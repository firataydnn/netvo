Nuuly accelerates order sorting automation at Kansas City fulfillment hub

4 Eyl 2026

Urban Outfitters' rental subscription service Nuuly is deploying automated sortation and picking systems at its Kansas City-area warehouse.

Nuuly, the fashion rental subscription service owned by Urban Outfitters, is advancing automation efforts at its Kansas City-region fulfillment center. The company plans to install an automated order sortation system and picking solution designed to streamline warehouse operations. This investment targets cost reduction and processing speed for the fast-turnaround subscription model.

Automation investments in fulfillment centers have become increasingly common across e-commerce and recurring subscription services. Rising order volumes, labor costs, and evolving customer expectations are pushing retailers toward warehouse technology upgrades. For time-sensitive rental models like Nuuly, automated sorting and picking systems can significantly accelerate the operational cycle—reducing the time between order receipt and shipment.

For Nuuly, this technology deployment aims to strengthen unit economics of the subscription business. Automating warehouse processes reduces return processing times and handling errors, which directly impacts profitability in a rental model. For e-commerce sellers and marketplace operators, similar systems improve inventory management and fulfillment accuracy. In the rental and subscription segment, warehouse automation is increasingly becoming a competitive necessity rather than a luxury investment.
