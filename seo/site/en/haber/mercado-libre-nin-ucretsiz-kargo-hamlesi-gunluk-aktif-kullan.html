Mercado Libre's Free Shipping Strategy Drives Up Daily Active Users

6 Ağu 2026

Following its reduction of the free shipping threshold in Brazil a year ago, Mercado Libre reported in Q2 results that the move has created a "structural change in behavior," with daily active user growth now outpacing monthly shopper growt

Mercado Libre has announced significant shifts in user behavior following its decision to lower the free shipping threshold in Brazil a year earlier. The Latin American ecommerce and fintech platform disclosed in its second-quarter earnings report released this week that the policy change has generated a "structural change in behavior" among its user base. Notably, the growth rate of daily active users has begun to exceed the growth rate of monthly active shoppers since the initiative took effect.

Free shipping has emerged as a pivotal lever in ecommerce marketplace strategies worldwide. In competitive markets like Brazil, where logistics costs directly impact basket value and purchase intent, reducing shipping thresholds can reshape purchasing patterns significantly. Mercado Libre's approach targets the conversion of occasional, monthly shoppers into more frequent daily users, thereby increasing total transaction volume and platform stickiness. This mirrors similar moves by Amazon, Shopee, and Lazada, underscoring how fulfillment economics have become central to marketplace growth strategies in emerging markets.

For merchants on the platform, increased transaction frequency translates to broader exposure and the ability to move lower-value items. Yet the structural economics raise questions about sustainability: the cost of free shipping will likely require either improved logistics partner terms or adjustments to seller commission structures. Further details in Mercado Libre's earnings disclosure should clarify how the company is managing these unit economics and whether the strategy is yielding profitable daily user engagement over the longer term.
