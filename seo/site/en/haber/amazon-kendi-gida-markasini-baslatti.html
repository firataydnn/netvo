Amazon launches its own grocery brand

1 Eki 2025

Most products under Amazon Fresh brand will be priced below $5 and sold both online and in Amazon Fresh stores.

Amazon is introducing a new grocery brand offering competitively priced products to consumers. The majority of items in the Amazon Fresh lineup will be positioned under the $5 price point, targeting value-conscious shoppers. These products will be distributed through both Amazon's e-commerce platform and its physical Amazon Fresh retail locations. The move strengthens Amazon's position against traditional grocery retailers by leveraging its omnichannel presence.
