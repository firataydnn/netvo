43% of Consumers Want Digital Wallets in Their Future AI-Powered Shopping

10 Eyl 2026

New research shows that 43% of consumers prefer digital wallet payments in artificial intelligence-enhanced shopping experiences, highlighting the critical need for merchants to optimize payment options at checkout.

A well-stocked shopping cart can lose momentum when customers encounter friction during payment. Retailers invest heavily to capture consumer interest, only to face cart abandonment at the final transaction stage. Recent data underscores how payment method availability directly influences purchase completion rates and overall customer satisfaction in online retail.

According to emerging research, 43% of consumers expect digital wallet payment options to be available in future online shopping experiences that incorporate artificial intelligence and advanced personalization. This preference reflects a broader shift in consumer expectations, particularly as e-commerce platforms integrate AI-driven recommendations and dynamic pricing. Digital wallets address multiple pain points—faster transaction processing, reduced friction, enhanced security, and seamless cross-device functionality—making them increasingly central to the modern checkout experience.

For merchants and marketplace operators, this data signals that checkout optimization is no longer a technical enhancement but a competitive necessity. Digital wallet integration has become a proven lever for reducing cart abandonment and improving payment success rates. The impact is especially pronounced in mobile commerce and social selling environments, where one-click payment solutions significantly boost conversion metrics. Forward-thinking platforms are expanding payment method diversity while prioritizing digital wallet compatibility to align with demonstrated customer preferences and capture sales that might otherwise be lost at the transaction stage.
