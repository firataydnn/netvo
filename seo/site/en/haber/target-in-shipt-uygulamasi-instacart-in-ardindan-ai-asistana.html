Target's Shipt Launches AI Assistant as Same-Day Delivery Competition Heats Up

17 Eyl 2026

Shipt, Target's delivery app, rolled out an AI-powered assistant in sync with rival Instacart's comparable move this week.

Shipt, the delivery application owned by Target and central to its same-day fulfillment offering, unveiled an artificial intelligence assistant last week—timed closely with a similar announcement from major competitor Instacart. As one of the leading platforms in accelerated retail delivery across the United States, Shipt is integrating AI capabilities to streamline customer service interactions and order processing workflows.

The move reflects an emerging pattern in the same-day delivery sector. Platforms like Instacart have begun embedding AI-powered assistants to personalize user experiences and reduce friction in the purchasing flow. Traditional large retailers such as Target are simultaneously fortifying their proprietary fulfillment networks, effectively blurring the line between e-commerce platforms and brick-and-mortar operations. Shipt's AI assistant is engineered to handle real-time product recommendations, answer customer inquiries, and automate product discovery during the ordering process.

For sellers and business operators, this trend underscores the critical importance of platform visibility and adaptation to new discoverability mechanisms. As AI-driven assistants learn customer preferences and behavior patterns, product discovery dynamics will shift. Additionally, as platforms employ AI tools to measure seller and operational performance metrics, standards for rating and rankings may become more automated and nuanced. With same-day delivery speed and reliability solidifying as key factors in online shopping decisions, such platform-level innovations are reshaping competitive boundaries in e-commerce.
