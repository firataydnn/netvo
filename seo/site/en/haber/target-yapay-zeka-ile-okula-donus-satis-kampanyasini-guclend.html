Target harnesses AI to boost back-to-school sales strategy

3 Eyl 2026

The major U.S. retailer is deploying artificial intelligence to power personalized product recommendations and smart wish lists on its e-commerce platform.

Target, one of America's largest retail chains, has begun deploying artificial intelligence at scale to enhance customer experience on its digital platform. According to the company's senior vice president of technology, AI has been integrated to deliver personalized product recommendations and enable customers to create dynamic wish lists during the crucial back-to-school shopping season. The technology is designed to deepen customer engagement and drive conversion rates during peak retail periods.

The adoption of AI in retail recommendation systems reflects a broader industry trend. Major e-commerce platforms increasingly embed machine learning models into search, ranking, and recommendation engines to better anticipate customer behavior and preferences. Target's move aligns with similar strategies deployed by competitors like Amazon and Shopify, underscoring how AI-driven personalization has become a competitive necessity in large-scale retail. Seasonal campaigns such as back-to-school shopping are particularly effective proving grounds for AI systems that identify high-intent customer segments.

For e-commerce sellers and marketplace participants, this development signals that organic product discoverability will become even more algorithm-dependent. AI-powered recommendations determine which products surface to which customers, potentially affecting visibility for less-prominent sellers and smaller brands. Sellers should prioritize data optimization—ensuring detailed product descriptions, accurate categorization, and complete attribute information—to improve their chances of being recommended by AI systems. Furthermore, as major platforms like Target invest in proprietary AI capabilities, smaller retailers and marketplace operators may face pressure to implement comparable technologies to remain competitive in an increasingly AI-mediated shopping environment.
