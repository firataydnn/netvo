E-commerce This Week — 21 Ağu–29 Ağu 2026

29 Ağu 2026

The week's most notable developments in e-commerce.

Standout headlines in e-commerce this week:

• YouTube integrates Amazon into shopping affiliate program — Amazon.com has joined YouTube's Shopping Affiliate Program, enabling eligible U.S. creators to tag Amazon products directly in Shorts, videos, and livestreams for instant purchasing.

• Amazon Pan-EU FBA expands to Netherlands and Belgium — Amazon has extended its Pan-European fulfillment network to include warehouses in the Netherlands and Belgium, enabling sellers to stock inventory across a wider European region.

• Tariff savings should flow to lower prices, says Burlington CEO — Burlington's chief executive views passing tariff relief into consumer discounts as both financially sound and necessary to support customers amid economic headwinds.

• Tariff relief lifts big retailers' margins, not shopper prices — Large US retailers are pocketing tariff refunds and customs savings while consumers face unchanged or rising prices at checkout.

• Temu accelerates European sellers' expansion into international markets — Half of European businesses selling on Temu report increased capacity or new hires; one-third have successfully entered new international markets.

• Shein loses copyright case against Temu — A court ruling against Shein's design infringement claim spotlights the legal boundaries of rapid product cycles in fast-fashion marketplaces.

• Temu strengthens local seller network to navigate EU regulatory pressures — Facing heightened EU oversight, Temu is accelerating recruitment and support for local merchants to align its supply chain with tariff policy shifts and compliance requirements.

• FTC Scrutinizes Personalized Pricing in E-Commerce — The U.S. Federal Trade Commission is investigating personalized pricing practices that show different prices to different customers.

• Furniture retailer Povison sees AI-driven traffic as key growth opportunity — E-commerce company Povison announced that visitor traffic from AI search and generative platforms is outpacing expectations, with plans to expand this channel significantly during the holiday season.

• Target's beauty section becomes gateway for emerging brands — Following its split with Ulta Beauty, Target's new beauty section has welcomed hundreds of brands entering traditional retail distribution for the first time.

• eBay Completes Depop Acquisition, Integrates C2C Fashion Into Main Platform — eBay begins integrating its completed Depop acquisition into eBay.com's fashion section, promoting the C2C marketplace to its core user base.

• TikTok Shop sales momentum boosts Amazon performance for brands — Brands are leveraging TikTok Shop as a strategic sales channel to drive growth on Amazon and other established marketplaces.

• EU customs rules put UK retailers' profitability to the test this Black Friday 2026 — New EU import duties on low-value ecommerce goods will pressure UK retailers' margins for the first time during peak trading season, raising questions about sustainable profitability.
