94% of TikTok Shop luxury resale revenue in the U.S. now comes from livestreams

16 Eyl 2026

TikTok revealed that nearly all revenue from luxury resale transactions on its U.S. marketplace is driven by live shopping broadcasts.

TikTok Shop disclosed a significant trend in its U.S. marketplace operations during a media presentation at New York Fashion Week: 94% of year-to-date revenue from luxury resale transactions originates from livestream sales. The figure underscores the dominant role that live shopping formats have assumed within the social commerce segment, particularly for higher-value, pre-owned luxury goods.

The statistic reflects a broader shift in how consumers discover and purchase premium secondhand items online. Livestream commerce, which combines real-time product presentation, interactive seller engagement, and social proof, has become the dominant sales channel for this category on TikTok Shop. The platform's ability to drive conversion through live formats in the luxury resale space demonstrates the model's effectiveness compared to static product listings and traditional marketplace browsing.

For sellers and marketplace operators, the data signals a clear strategic imperative: livestream capabilities are now essential infrastructure for competing in high-value resale categories. Brands and resellers leveraging TikTok Shop's live features report stronger engagement metrics and transaction volumes. As the luxury resale market continues to expand, the platform's findings suggest that competitors must enhance their own live shopping offerings to remain competitive in capturing this growing consumer segment.
