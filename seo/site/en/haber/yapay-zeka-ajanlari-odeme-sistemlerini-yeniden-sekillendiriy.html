AI Agents Drive New Requirements in Payment Infrastructure

18 Eyl 2026

Stockholm-based fintech SolvaPay is developing payment infrastructure designed specifically for autonomous AI agents to conduct purchases, introducing fresh security and operational challenges to the payments ecosystem.

For years, the payments industry has invested heavily in fraud detection systems designed to prevent bots from impersonating legitimate customers. Yet the emergence of agentic commerce introduces a fundamental shift: some bots now need to actually complete transactions. Companies like SolvaPay are responding by building payment platforms purpose-built to support AI agents as genuine economic actors.

The integration of AI agents into e-commerce raises critical questions about how traditional payment verification and fraud prevention work. Current systems rely on behavioral patterns tied to human customers, making automated decision-making straightforward and rules-based. AI agents, however, operate with different transaction patterns, speeds, and decision logic that existing infrastructure was not designed to accommodate. SolvaPay's initiatives in this space reflect how advanced the intersection of AI and commerce has become.

For e-commerce operators and payment providers, this shift carries dual implications. On one hand, enabling AI agents to transact opens new revenue streams, simplifies B2B automation, and can reduce friction in supply chain transactions. On the other, distinguishing genuine human customers from autonomous agents, managing fraud detection, and maintaining security becomes considerably more complex. Payment systems will need to evolve rapidly to support this new category of buyer while maintaining robust protective measures.
