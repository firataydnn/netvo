Walmart transforms stores as e-commerce reshapes retail landscape

20 Ağu 2026

Walmart's CFO emphasizes that physical stores remain central to omnichannel retail strategy amid e-commerce growth.

Walmart is reimagining its traditional store network in response to rapid e-commerce expansion. The company's Chief Financial Officer John David Rainey stressed that brick-and-mortar locations are integral to an omnichannel strategy, enabling services like buy-online-pickup-in-store and local fulfillment. The shift underscores how legacy retailers are adapting to compete in the digital age; Walmart's second-quarter U.S. sales performance was also tempered by pharmaceutical pricing regulations.
