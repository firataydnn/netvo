Yuno and Revolut partnership streamlines merchant payment stack integration

4 Eyl 2026

Payment solutions platform Yuno has partnered with Revolut Business to allow merchants to access Revolut's biometric checkout, payment services, and acquiring capabilities through a single connection.

Yuno has announced a new partnership with Revolut Business that simplifies how merchants integrate multiple payment solutions. Under this collaboration, sellers can now connect to Revolut's biometric one-click checkout technology, Revolut Pay service, and acquiring and gateway functionalities through a single Yuno integration point.

The partnership addresses a persistent challenge in e-commerce operations: the fragmentation of payment infrastructure. Traditionally, merchants must negotiate and implement separate integrations with each payment provider, a process that consumes technical resources and extends time-to-market. By consolidating these connections through Yuno's platform layer, the new offering reduces integration complexity and accelerates deployment for sellers of all sizes.

For merchants, this development carries meaningful implications for conversion optimization and customer experience. Fast checkout methods like biometric authentication lower cart abandonment while streamlining the purchase journey. The inclusion of Revolut Pay expands payment choice in regions where digital wallets and alternative payment methods have gained significant traction, particularly across Europe. The partnership reflects broader industry momentum toward payment infrastructure consolidation, where platforms increasingly bundle multiple services to reduce operational friction for sellers.
