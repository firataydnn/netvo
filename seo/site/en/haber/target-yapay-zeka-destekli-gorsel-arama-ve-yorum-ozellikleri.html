Target rolls out AI-powered visual search and enhanced review features

10 Eyl 2026

Target has introduced artificial intelligence-driven photo search and improved review systems across its mobile app and website to strengthen product discovery and personalization capabilities.

Target is deploying new artificial intelligence capabilities on its digital platforms, including visual search functionality and an upgraded review management system. The visual search feature allows customers to photograph a product with their smartphone and discover similar items in Target's catalog. The enhanced review system uses AI to filter, rank, and summarize customer feedback more intelligently, delivering more relevant and useful information to potential buyers. These tools are designed to improve product discovery and boost conversion rates.

This move reflects a broader industry trend in which major retailers invest heavily in AI to enhance user experience and competitive positioning. Amazon, Google Shopping, and Shopify-enabled merchants have already launched comparable visual and AI-powered discovery tools. Target's rollout underscores how legacy and online-first retailers are leveraging machine learning to close the gap in digital-first commerce capabilities.

For e-commerce sellers, these changes carry operational implications. High-quality product photography and detailed descriptions become increasingly critical, as AI-powered visual search algorithms prioritize clear, well-lit images and comprehensive metadata. Additionally, as AI systems rank and highlight reviews more prominently, sellers must focus intensely on product quality and customer satisfaction to generate positive feedback that algorithms will surface. Looking ahead, AI-driven discovery and review intelligence will likely become table-stakes for competitive advantage on major U.S. retail platforms.
