Amazon Pan-EU FBA expands to Netherlands and Belgium

28 Ağu 2026

Amazon has extended its Pan-European fulfillment network to include warehouses in the Netherlands and Belgium, enabling sellers to stock inventory across a wider European region.

Amazon has announced the addition of the Netherlands and Belgium to its Pan-European FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) network. This expansion allows e-commerce sellers operating in Europe to distribute their products across a broader geographic footprint. Once sellers ship inventory to an EU fulfillment center, Amazon's system automatically manages stock placement across enabled locations within its European distribution network.

Pan-EU FBA has become a cornerstone tool for cross-border selling in Europe. Rather than handling separate shipments to individual countries, sellers can leverage Amazon's centralized warehouse infrastructure to reduce shipping costs and accelerate delivery timelines. The inclusion of the Netherlands and Belgium, both major logistics hubs, strengthens supply chain efficiency and particularly benefits sellers targeting Central and Northern European markets with faster fulfillment capabilities.

For small and medium-sized sellers, this development translates into improved product availability across the continent and faster local delivery times. Additional warehouse nodes enhance logistics efficiency while also streamlining returns and exchange processes. Amazon's network expansion strengthens seller competitiveness while positioning the platform for deeper market penetration in the region.
