FAQ

How much is PttAVM's commission?

PttAVM commission ranges from %5–15 by category. The rate includes VAT.

How much is really deducted per sale on PttAVM?

On the advertised 12% commission, adding VAT and fixed fees brings the total marketplace deduction to about 12%.

What is the VAT rate for PttAVM?

Türkiye standard VAT is 20%. Commission base: price incl. VAT.
