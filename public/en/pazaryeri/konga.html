FAQ

How much is Konga (Nigeria)'s commission?

Konga (Nigeria) commission ranges from %5.8–20 by category. VAT is added on top of the rate.

How much is really deducted per sale on Konga?

On the advertised 10.5% commission, adding VAT and fixed fees brings the total marketplace deduction to about 11.3%.

What is the VAT rate for Konga (Nigeria)?

Nigeria standard VAT is 7.5%. Commission base: price incl. VAT.
