FAQ

How much is Amazon TR's commission?

Amazon TR commission ranges from %7–20 by category. VAT is added on top of the rate.

How much is really deducted per sale on Amazon TR?

On the advertised 12.6% commission, adding VAT and fixed fees brings the total marketplace deduction to about 15.1%.

What is the VAT rate for Amazon TR?

Türkiye standard VAT is 20%. Commission base: price incl. VAT.
