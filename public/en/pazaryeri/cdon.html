FAQ

How much is CDON (İskandinav)'s commission?

CDON (İskandinav) commission ranges from %4–12 by category. VAT is added on top of the rate.

How much is really deducted per sale on CDON?

On the advertised 8% commission, adding VAT and fixed fees brings the total marketplace deduction to about 10%.

What is the VAT rate for CDON (İskandinav)?

Sweden standard VAT is 25%. Commission base: price incl. VAT.
