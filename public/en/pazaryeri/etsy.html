FAQ

How much is Etsy (Global)'s commission?

Etsy (Global) commission is %6.5. VAT is added on top of the rate.

How much is really deducted per sale on Etsy?

On the advertised 6.5% commission, adding VAT and fixed fees brings the total marketplace deduction to about 6.6%.

What is the VAT rate for Etsy (Global)?

Global standard VAT is 0%. Commission base: price incl. VAT.
