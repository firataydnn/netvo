Nigeria's E-Commerce Set to Outpace Global Growth Average

1 Eyl 2026

According to logistics firm DHL, Nigeria is positioned for above-average e-commerce expansion compared to worldwide figures.

Nigeria's e-commerce sector is emerging as a growth hotspot, with expansion forecasts exceeding the global average. International logistics and shipping provider DHL's analysis suggests that online retail activity in West Africa's largest economy is poised to accelerate at rates surpassing those recorded in other regions worldwide.

With a population exceeding 220 million and rising internet penetration, Nigeria represents a market with substantial untapped potential. Mobile-first shopping behaviors are becoming entrenched, payment solutions are diversifying, and local merchants and entrepreneurs are increasingly adopting marketplace platforms. The strategic focus of global logistics operators like DHL on the region underscores the recognition of this opportunity.

For sellers and platform operators active in Nigeria, this trajectory signals growing market confidence and investment potential. Expanding transaction volumes will drive demand for cargo and last-mile delivery services, making upgrades to logistics infrastructure and customs clearance procedures increasingly urgent. Regional payment network expansion and simplified cross-border e-commerce arrangements will be essential enablers of sustained growth.
