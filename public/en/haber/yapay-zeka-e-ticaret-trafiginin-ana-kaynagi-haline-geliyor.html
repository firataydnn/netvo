AI Becomes Major Driver of E-Commerce Referral Traffic

3 Eyl 2026

According to Adobe Analytics, AI-sourced referral traffic to e-commerce sites surged 62% year-over-year in July 2026, fundamentally reshaping online retail traffic sources.

Artificial intelligence systems have emerged as a rapidly growing source of referral traffic to e-commerce platforms. Adobe Analytics data shows that AI-generated referral traffic increased by 62% year-over-year in July 2026, demonstrating the accelerating power of AI tools in directing consumers to online retailers. This figure underscores how quickly AI-powered solutions are becoming competitors to traditional search engines and social media channels in driving commerce.

The sharp rise in AI referral traffic correlates directly with the mainstream adoption of large language models like ChatGPT and Claude, many of which now feature integrated shopping capabilities. Consumers increasingly use AI assistants for product research alongside traditional search results, prompting retailers to prioritize visibility and optimization across multiple AI platforms. The shift reflects a fundamental change in how shoppers discover and navigate to e-commerce sites.

For online sellers and e-commerce operations, this trend signals a major reallocation of marketing resources. Visibility in AI search interfaces—such as ChatGPT's plugin ecosystem, Claude, and similar platforms—has become as critical as traditional Google Search and social media advertising. While SEO and paid search remain important, ignoring AI-driven traffic channels puts retailers at a competitive disadvantage.

Industry analysts expect this trajectory to accelerate through 2026 and beyond. E-commerce businesses now must ensure their product data, descriptions, and catalogs are discoverable and optimized for AI systems. Companies that adapt quickly to this new paradigm are positioned to capture substantial market share as AI becomes the default discovery channel for growing segments of online shoppers.
