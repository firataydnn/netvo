Instacart and Shipt roll out AI-powered shopping assistants

9 Eyl 2026

The two major grocery delivery platforms launched artificial intelligence tools enabling customers to build shopping carts through conversation or uploaded photos.

Instacart and Shipt have introduced AI-powered shopping assistants designed to streamline the customer shopping experience. The new feature allows users to build a grocery cart either by conversing with the assistant or uploading a photo taken with their smartphone camera. The system automatically identifies and organizes products into relevant categories, reducing manual effort and speeding up the purchasing process.

Artificial intelligence integration in online grocery retail is becoming increasingly common as platforms seek to differentiate their offerings. This move by Instacart and Shipt reflects a broader trend in e-commerce where automation simplifies product discovery and transaction completion. The technology targets efficiency gains by removing friction from routine tasks like list creation and product categorization, making the shopping journey faster and more intuitive for end users.

For merchants and platform operators, this development represents an opportunity to boost conversion rates and customer satisfaction simultaneously. AI-assisted search and recommendation systems typically drive better product visibility and higher average cart values. As customers complete their purchases more quickly and with less friction, repeat purchase rates tend to improve. However, sellers must ensure their product data, descriptions, and categorizations are optimized for AI recognition, and platforms should monitor user feedback closely to refine the assistant's accuracy over time.
