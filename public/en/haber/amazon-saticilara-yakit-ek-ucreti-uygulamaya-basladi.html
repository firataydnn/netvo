Amazon rolls out fuel surcharge for sellers amid energy volatility

2 Nis 2026

Amazon has introduced a fuel surcharge affecting sellers, citing temporary measures in response to global energy market shifts, with no clear end date announced.

Amazon has activated a fuel surcharge policy affecting its selling partners, characterizing it as a temporary adjustment without specifying when it will be discontinued. The company attributes the measure to volatility in global energy markets, citing regional tensions and rising operational costs. The surcharge represents a direct cost increase for sellers using Amazon's logistics and fulfillment services, potentially compressing margins for third-party merchants on the platform.
