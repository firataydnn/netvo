FTC orders nine companies including Walmart and Target to explain personal-data pricing

October 30, 2026

The 28 October orders open a 45-day study rather than a lawsuit, putting personalized pricing under scrutiny in the US.

According to the Paradise Report newsletter, the US Federal Trade Commission on 28 October ordered nine companies, including Walmart and Target, to explain how they use personal data to set prices. The orders start a 45-day study rather than a lawsuit. Because this is relayed through a secondary source, the details should be confirmed against the FTC's own announcement.

What it means for sellers: if you use customer-level or behavior-based dynamic pricing, you should be able to document which data influences prices and explain it transparently. Before running holiday price tests, it is prudent to review any rules that rely on personal data.
