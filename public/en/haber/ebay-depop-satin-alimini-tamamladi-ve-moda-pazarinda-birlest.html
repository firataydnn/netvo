eBay Completes Depop Acquisition, Integrates C2C Fashion Into Main Platform

26 Ağu 2026

eBay begins integrating its completed Depop acquisition into eBay.com's fashion section, promoting the C2C marketplace to its core user base.

eBay has completed its acquisition of Depop and is now actively integrating the secondhand fashion marketplace into its core platform experience. The company has placed prominent banner advertisements on eBay.com's Fashion category, introducing mainstream eBay shoppers to Depop's consumer-to-consumer model and inventory.

Depop is a social fashion marketplace particularly popular with Gen Z and younger buyers seeking pre-owned and trendy apparel. eBay's acquisition reflects the platform's strategic push to reach younger demographics and capture growth in the rapidly expanding secondhand and sustainable fashion segment. This move positions eBay to compete more effectively in a market where alternative C2C platforms have gained significant traction.

For sellers and fashion merchants, this integration signals a reshaping of eBay's marketplace ecosystem and user engagement strategy. The Depop integration indicates that fashion will receive heightened prominence across eBay's platform, with C2C fashion options now exposed to eBay's established customer base. Sellers should expect evolving inventory opportunities and potential category strategy shifts as both platforms converge.
