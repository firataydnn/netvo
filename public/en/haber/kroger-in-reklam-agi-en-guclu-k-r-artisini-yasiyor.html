Kroger's advertising business hits strongest profit growth since 2021

11 Eyl 2026

The major U.S. grocery chain reported its best advertising revenue profit growth in two years as retail media networks become a critical revenue driver.

Kroger, one of the nation's largest retail chains, achieved its strongest profit growth from its advertising business since 2021. The company's retail media network enables brands and product marketers to purchase ad placements across shelf displays, search results, and digital storefronts within its store environment and online platforms. This milestone reflects the broader shift among traditional retailers toward monetizing their direct customer relationships and in-store traffic through proprietary advertising networks.

The acceleration of Kroger's ad business mirrors similar moves by retail giants Amazon and Walmart, which have built highly profitable advertising divisions by leveraging their vast store and customer data. These platforms use first-party shopping behavior and demographics to help brands target consumers at the moment of purchase intent. For retail media networks, the economics are compelling: high margins, reduced dependence on merchandise sales alone, and recurring advertiser budgets. The segment has become one of the most profitable for large retailers.

For third-party sellers and brands, Kroger's expanding ad platform represents both opportunity and challenge. Increasing ad spend on Kroger's network means dividing budgets across multiple platforms—Amazon, Walmart, Instacart, and traditional search engines. As advertising becomes more central to visibility within major retail chains, smaller vendors face pressure to allocate marketing budgets strategically. The trend also signals that product discoverability and shelf space are increasingly earned through paid advertising rather than traditional retailer relationships, reshaping how brands compete and price products.
