Ocean shipping peak season extends beyond industry forecasts

14 Eyl 2026

According to the National Retail Federation and Hackett Associates, container shipments across major ocean routes are expected to peak in September, contradicting earlier predictions of an earlier-than-usual end to the busy season.

Ocean freight shipments are maintaining peak season intensity longer than industry analysts had anticipated. Data from the National Retail Federation and Hackett Associates indicates that container volumes crossing major trade routes are reaching their highest levels in September, defying earlier forecasts that the seasonal surge would taper off sooner. Demand patterns have proven resilient across import channels, keeping rates elevated and capacity tight.

The extended peak season translates into sustained pressure on logistics costs for e-commerce operators and traditional retailers alike. Container rates and vessel capacity remain premium-priced as the busy window persists beyond expectations. Warehouse availability stays constrained, and securing reliable shipping slots remains competitive, pushing operational expenses higher for businesses dependent on ocean freight.

For e-commerce sellers and supply chain managers, the prolonged high-cost period necessitates adjustments to inventory planning and replenishment schedules. The overlap with back-to-school and early holiday shopping windows has created stronger-than-typical demand momentum. Operators should expect elevated shipping expenses to remain in effect through at least the end of the following month.
