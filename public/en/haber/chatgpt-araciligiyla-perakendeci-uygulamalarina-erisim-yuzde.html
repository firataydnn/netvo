ChatGPT referrals to retail apps surge 28% year-over-year

2 Ara 2025

User traffic driven by AI to e-commerce apps rose significantly, with Walmart and Amazon capturing the largest share of gains.

Referrals from ChatGPT to retailer applications climbed 28 percent compared to the prior year, with particularly pronounced growth during the Black Friday period. Walmart and Amazon captured the lion's share of this AI-driven traffic surge, a dynamic reflecting how consumers now leverage conversational AI to discover products and make purchasing decisions. ChatGPT and similar tools have effectively become discovery and navigation channels within the broader e-commerce ecosystem, influencing how shoppers transition from research to purchase.

This shift carries profound strategic implications for the retail sector. While traditional search engines and social platforms remain significant traffic sources, AI-mediated referrals now represent a material channel that established retailers are positioned to capture. The concentration of gains among Walmart and Amazon stems from their existing AI infrastructure, extensive product catalogs, and seamless app integrations—advantages that smaller and mid-market e-commerce businesses cannot yet match. The emerging data suggests a widening competitive moat for large platforms while smaller retailers confront a new hurdle in customer acquisition.

Looking forward, this trajectory will likely accelerate as AI assistants become more sophisticated. E-commerce operators must now prioritize optimizing product data visibility within AI platforms, enhancing app discoverability, and refining user experience pathways. Companies that fail to adapt their distribution strategies may find themselves increasingly isolated from this growing channel—making AI integration not merely strategic but essential for competitive survival in the evolving digital commerce landscape.
