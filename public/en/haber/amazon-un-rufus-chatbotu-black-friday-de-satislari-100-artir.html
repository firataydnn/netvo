Amazon's Rufus AI chatbot drove 100% sales lift on Black Friday

1 Ara 2025

Amazon's AI-powered Rufus chatbot doubled sales conversion rates when used during Black Friday, compared to 20% growth without the assistant.

During Amazon's Black Friday campaign, sessions where customers used the AI chatbot Rufus achieved significantly higher purchase conversion rates than those without the tool. Transactions assisted by Rufus showed a 100% sales increase, while sessions without the chatbot support recorded only a 20% lift. This performance highlights how Amazon's artificial intelligence investment directly impacts customer purchasing decisions, particularly during peak shopping periods when decision fatigue and product complexity pose the greatest obstacles to completing sales.

For e-commerce sellers and business operators, these findings carry substantial implications for competitive strategy. Real-time AI assistance not only improves customer satisfaction but directly translates into measurable revenue gains. The data underscores that intelligent checkout support substantially enhances conversion rates, especially when helping customers navigate intricate product selections and comparisons. As retailers observe these results, many are likely to accelerate their own AI initiatives, potentially making sophisticated chatbot technology an industry standard rather than a differentiator in the coming years.
