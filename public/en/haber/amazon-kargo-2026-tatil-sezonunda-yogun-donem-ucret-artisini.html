Amazon Shipping Raises Peak Season Delivery Fees for 2026 Holiday Period

3 Eyl 2026

Amazon Shipping will implement higher surcharges during peak holiday season, with the steepest rates running from November 22 through December 26, marking a year-over-year increase.

Amazon Shipping has announced elevated peak season surcharges for the 2026 holiday shipping period. The additional fees will reach their peak between November 22 and December 26, with rates climbing higher than the previous year's levels. The carrier is signaling cost pressures building into the upcoming critical retail season.

Peak season surcharges are a standard industry practice. During the holiday surge, package volumes can spike tenfold, forcing logistics providers to add warehouse capacity, extend operating hours, and hire temporary staff. Amazon's decision to increase its peak-period rates reflects both elevated demand expectations and rising operational costs in its fulfillment network.

Third-party sellers using Amazon's FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) will feel the direct impact on their bottom line. With holiday selling season offering high revenue potential but compressed margins due to elevated shipping costs, many merchants will need to recalculate profitability projections. Some may face difficult choices: absorb higher fees, pass costs to customers through price increases, or explore alternative fulfillment models.

Announcing peak season rates several months ahead allows sellers time to model scenarios and adjust strategy. Historical patterns suggest merchants are already evaluating whether to remain on FBA, shift to Merchant Fulfilled Network alternatives, negotiate rates with third-party carriers, or adjust product pricing to offset logistics inflation during Q4.
