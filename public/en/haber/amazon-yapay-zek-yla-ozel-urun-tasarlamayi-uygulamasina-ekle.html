Amazon adds AI-designed custom merchandise to its app

8 Haz 2026

A new Shopping-app feature turns Alexa-generated designs into printed products like T-shirts.

Amazon has added a feature that lets users create custom merchandise with AI inside its Shopping app. Shoppers can generate designs through Alexa and print them on products such as T-shirts, hoodies and tumblers. The feature builds personalization directly into the shopping flow, expanding on-platform creation.
