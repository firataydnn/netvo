Amazon expands Shop Direct program to more retailers

11 Mar 2026

Amazon broadens access to its Shop Direct initiative, allowing more merchants to direct customers to competing retailers' websites.

Amazon is expanding its Shop Direct program, a feature that enables more merchants to direct their customers to other retailers' product pages and earn commissions from this traffic. The program allows participating sellers to diversify their revenue streams by routing customers to competitor platforms, particularly when products are out of stock or available at better prices elsewhere. This flexibility enables merchants to maintain customer relationships even when they cannot fulfill orders directly, effectively transforming them into affiliate marketers while they remain Amazon vendors.

From a market perspective, this initiative signals Amazon's confidence in its core platform strength and its commitment to seller retention through innovative incentive structures. The program generates additional commission revenue for Amazon while positioning the platform as a facilitator of the broader e-commerce ecosystem rather than merely a gatekeeper. For sellers, Shop Direct represents a strategic tool to optimize customer lifetime value and create multiple monetization points. Looking ahead, this model may influence how other platforms approach seller relationships, potentially normalizing affiliate-style partnerships as standard features within larger e-commerce marketplaces.
