TikTok Rolls Out Amazon Prime-Style Membership Program Test

26 Tem 2026

TikTok has begun testing a subscription-based shopping membership called TikTok Shop Plus in the U.S., designed to offer shoppers and sellers premium benefits.

TikTok is testing a membership service that mirrors Amazon Prime's model, integrating it directly into its e-commerce platform. According to Business Insider's report on Friday, the initiative is called TikTok Shop Plus and aims to create a subscription tier for U.S. shoppers. Screenshots reviewed by journalists show the program is in active development within TikTok's broader e-commerce ecosystem, though full feature details have not been publicly disclosed.

This move underscores TikTok's evolution from a social platform into a full-fledged marketplace operator. The company has rapidly expanded TikTok Shop across multiple markets, investing heavily in live shopping and short-form video commerce. Subscription models have proven successful for established e-commerce players like Amazon and Walmart, offering both customer loyalty mechanisms and recurring revenue streams. TikTok's decision to test a similar approach signals confidence in the durability of subscription-based commerce and the appetite of its user base for dedicated shopping benefits.

For sellers, a membership tier could translate to access to a larger cohort of repeat customers and potential higher average order values. Members may receive perks such as discounted shipping, expedited delivery, or exclusive seller promotions, incentivizing continued purchasing. Buyers, in turn, might gain benefits ranging from membership fees waived for first orders to flexible return policies or category-specific discounts. Exact offerings remain unclear at this stage, but the testing phase suggests TikTok is preparing for a full rollout.

The program's success will depend on takeup rates, competitive pricing against existing alternatives, and TikTok's ability to execute operationally. If the U.S. test gains traction, similar membership tiers could expand to TikTok Shop regions in Southeast Asia, Latin America, and Europe. This strategy also positions TikTok to compete more directly with Amazon's premium membership model while leveraging its core strength in social and entertainment to drive commerce adoption.
