Walmart invests $1.3B in automated fulfillment hub in Georgia

28 Ağu 2026

Walmart is building a 1.5-million-square-foot automated fulfillment center in Georgia with a $1.3 billion investment, with groundbreaking slated for later this year.

Walmart is expanding its logistics network in the U.S. Southeast by constructing a new 1.5-million-square-foot fulfillment center in Georgia, backed by a $1.3 billion investment. Construction is expected to begin later this year. The facility will incorporate advanced automation technologies to streamline package sorting and order processing.

This expansion aligns with a broader industry trend of major e-commerce and retail players investing heavily in warehouse automation and regional logistics hubs. As online shopping demand remains elevated and customer expectations for faster delivery times persist, traditional retailers are strengthening their own fulfillment infrastructure to compete with pure-play e-commerce operators. Last-mile delivery cost management has become a central focus, driving automation-heavy warehouse builds across North America.

For marketplace sellers and third-party vendors using Walmart's fulfillment services, this new facility could translate to faster order processing, improved inventory turnover in high-demand regions, and potentially lower fulfillment fees over time. Automated facilities typically reduce per-unit handling costs and increase throughput, which may benefit smaller sellers leveraging Walmart's logistics network. However, large-scale automation plays like this one can widen the operational and cost advantages of major retailers, making it harder for smaller or independent perakendecilerin competitors to match service levels and speed.
